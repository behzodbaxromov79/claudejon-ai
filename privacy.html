Privacy Policy — Claudejon AI

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What we access

When you connect your YouTube channel to the YouTube Agent in @Claudejon_ai_bot, you grant access through Google's OAuth screen. We use this access only to read your channel's name and ID (to show which channel is connected), to upload videos that you ordered to your channel as private, and to change a video to public only when you press "Publish" in the bot.

What we store

Your Telegram user ID, the connected channel's ID and title, and an OAuth refresh token, stored on our server and used only for the actions above. We do not read your private videos, comments, messages or analytics, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

How to remove access

You can disconnect at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (remove "Claudejon AI"), or with the "Disconnect YouTube" button in the bot. When you disconnect in the bot, we delete your stored token.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
