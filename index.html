Claudejon AI — YouTube va Instagram ijodkorlari uchun Telegram bot (@Claudejon_ai_bot). Bot video yasaydi, o'zbekcha subtitr va inglizcha dublyaj qiladi, kanal tahlili va g'oyalar beradi. «YouTube Agent» xizmati foydalanuvchining ruxsati bilan uning YouTube kanaliga tayyor videolarni yuklaydi — avval yopiq holda, foydalanuvchi tasdiqlagandan keyin e'lon qilinadi.

Claudejon AI is a Telegram bot (@Claudejon_ai_bot) for YouTube and Instagram creators. It makes videos, Uzbek subtitles and English dubbing, channel analysis and ideas. The "YouTube Agent" service uploads finished videos to the user's own YouTube channel only with the user's permission (uploaded as private first; published only after the user approves).
